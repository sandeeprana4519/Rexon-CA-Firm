Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Apex & Co. Chartered Accountants, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and financial information you entrust to us. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, storage, and processing of data collected through this website and our client consultation portals.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website—such as submitting a consultation request, callback request, appointment booking, or contact form—we collect basic identification and contact details, including:

  • Your Full Name and Organization Name
  • Mobile Number and WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • Service requirement categories (ITR, GST, Audit, Incorporation)
  • Preferred meeting dates and times
  • Specific inquiries or background notes submitted voluntarily in form text fields

2. Purpose of Information Collection

Submitted information is used exclusively to:

  • Evaluate and respond to your professional accounting and tax inquiries.
  • Schedule personal or video consultations with qualified partners.
  • Confirm appointment requests and provide callback services.
  • Comply with regulatory recordkeeping under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and relevant taxation statutes.

3. Data Security & Storage

All website form transmissions are encrypted via Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS). Data records are stored securely in Supabase with database-level Row Level Security (RLS) enforcement. Under no circumstances is your personal data rented, traded, or sold to third-party marketing companies.

4. Client Confidentiality

In accordance with the Code of Ethics established by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), all financial records, tax returns, balance sheets, and audit working papers are treated with strictest professional non-disclosure.
