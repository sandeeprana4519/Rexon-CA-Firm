Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common CA & Tax Questions

Clear, direct information on filing deadlines, documentation, and accounting compliance.

What documents are required for Income Tax Return (ITR) filing?
For individuals and salaried employees: PAN card, Aadhaar card, Form 16 from your employer, Form 26AS, Annual Information Statement (AIS/TIS), bank statements for all active accounts, and proofs of tax-saving investments (Section 80C, 80D, home loan interest certificates, capital gains statements). For business owners, audited or unaudited profit & loss accounts and balance sheets are also required.
Who needs mandatory GST registration in India?
GST registration is mandatory for businesses whose annual aggregate turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs for goods (₹20 Lakhs for special category states) and ₹20 Lakhs for service providers (₹10 Lakhs in special states). In addition, any business engaged in inter-state taxable supply, casual taxable persons, or selling through e-commerce portals (Amazon, Flipkart) must obtain GST registration irrespective of turnover.
How long does GST registration take?
Once all required documents (PAN, Aadhaar, proof of business address, electricity bill, rent agreement/NOC, and bank details) are verified and submitted with Aadhaar authentication, GST registration approval typically takes 3 to 7 working days from the GST department.
Can you handle ongoing monthly accounting and payroll?
Yes. We provide complete outsourced monthly bookkeeping, ledger entries, bank reconciliations, accounts payable/receivable management, monthly TDS computations, and payroll salary slip generation. You receive structured financial reports at the end of each month.
I received an Income Tax Notice. How can your firm help?
Do not panic. Notices under Section 143(1), 139(9) (defective return), 142(1), or 148 are common. Our Chartered Accountants inspect the underlying calculation error or mismatch in 26AS/AIS, draft a legally backed rectification or response on the e-filing portal, and represent your case before the assessing authority if necessary.

You can fill out the consultation form above, call our office directly, or click the WhatsApp button. Our team will coordinate an in-person meeting at our office or schedule a video conference via Google Meet / Zoom at your convenience.
