Income Tax & ITR Filing Services

Accurate computation, proactive tax planning, and strategic defense against tax notices for salaried individuals, high-net-worth investors, professionals, and corporate entities.

Strategic Income Tax Compliance & Advisory

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) is not just a statutory duty; it is a critical financial footprint that establishes your creditworthiness for home loans, business financing, visa applications, and foreign investments.

With the advent of the Annual Information Statement (AIS), Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS), and automated transaction tracking, income tax reporting requires meticulous reconciliation with Form 26AS, bank statements, and trading accounts. Our practice eliminates mismatches and optimizes your legitimate deductions under both Old and New Tax Regimes.

Key Benefits of Our Tax Advisory

Regime Optimization

Comparative analytical computation between Old vs New Tax Regimes to minimize your tax liability legally.

Capital Gains Planning

Exact indexation computation for real estate, mutual funds, listed equities, ESOPs, and Section 54/54EC exemptions.

Notice Protection

Complete alignment with AIS/TIS to prevent automated demand notices under Section 143(1) and 139(9).

Fast Refund Tracking

Prompt e-verification and pre-validated bank account linking for swift income tax refund crediting.

Who Needs This Service?

  • ✓ Salaried Individuals: Multiple employers, Form 16, HRA exemptions, home loan interest, and foreign assets.
  • ✓ Freelancers, Consultants & Doctors: Presumptive taxation under Section 44ADA with 50% deemed profit advantage.
  • ✓ Traders & Stock Market Investors: F&O transactions, intraday equity trading, and crypto asset capital gains computation.
  • ✓ Companies, LLPs & Firms: Corporate tax returns (ITR-6, ITR-5), MAT calculations, and tax audit compliance.

Documents Required for Filing

  • PAN Card & Aadhaar Card
  • Form 16 / Form 16A (TDS Certificates)
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS & TIS)
  • Bank statements for all accounts (April to March)
  • Capital gains statement from broker / CAMS
  • Home loan interest certificate & Property tax proofs
  • Proofs of Section 80C, 80D, 80G donations
  • Books of Accounts (P&L and Balance Sheet for businesses)
