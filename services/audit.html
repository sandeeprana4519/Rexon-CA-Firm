Rigorous, independent, and insightful Statutory, Tax, and Internal Audits designed to protect shareholders, satisfy statutory authorities, and elevate corporate governance.

Independent Verification Grounded in Technical Precision

An audit is more than a statutory mandate—it is a vital appraisal of your enterprise's financial integrity, risk management posture, and internal control effectiveness.

Our audit team strictly follows the Auditing and Assurance Standards (AAS) formulated by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) and complies with the reporting parameters of CARO 2020, Companies Act 2013, and Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act.

Our Key Audit Engagements

Statutory Audit

Independent financial statement audits for Private Limited, Public Limited, and Section 8 companies under the Companies Act 2013.

Tax Audit (Sec 44AB)

Mandatory audit of books of account under Section 44AB for businesses with turnover exceeding statutory limits, filing Form 3CA/3CB and Form 3CD.

Internal & Management Audit

Systemic review of operating procedures, fraud prevention mechanisms, purchase cycles, and internal financial controls (IFC).

Stock & Asset Verification

Physical inspection and verification of raw material inventory, finished stock, and fixed assets for bank limit compliance.

Statutory Tax Audit Thresholds (FY 2025-26)

  • Business with >5% cash transactions: Tax Audit is mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore.
  • Business with digital transactions (>95% non-cash): Tax Audit threshold is enhanced up to ₹10 Crores.
  • Professionals (Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Architects): Tax Audit is required if gross receipts exceed ₹50 Lakhs (or ₹75 Lakhs under 44ADA with digital transactions).
