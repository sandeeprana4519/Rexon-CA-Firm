Choosing the Right Business Structure in India

The structure you select impacts your personal liability, investor fundability, corporate tax rate, and ongoing compliance obligations under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

Our Chartered Accountants evaluate your capital structure, co-founder equity, and business model to recommend the most optimal legal vehicle—whether Private Limited Company, LLP, OPC, or Partnership.

Popular Entity Structures Compared

Private Limited Company

Gold standard for tech startups raising angel or VC capital. Limited liability, separate legal entity, ESOP eligibility.

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Best for professional services, agencies, and family businesses. Limited liability with reduced annual compliance overhead.

One Person Company (OPC)

Allows a solo founder to operate with corporate limited liability status without needing a second co-director.

MSME / Udyam & Startup India

Procuring Udyam registration and DPIIT recognition for tax holidays, collateral-free loans, and patent discounts.

Documents Required From Promoters

  • PAN Card of all Directors / Partners
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport (for NRI/Foreign nationals)
  • Bank Statement / Electricity Bill (<2 months old)
  • Registered Office Address proof (Electricity Bill / NOC)
  • Rent Agreement between owner and proposed company
  • Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) - arranged by us
