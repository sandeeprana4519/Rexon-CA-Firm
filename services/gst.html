What is GST & Why Compliance is Vital

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a unified multi-stage destination-based tax on the supply of goods and services in India. Operating without GST registration or defaulting on monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings leads to steep late fees (₹50/day), 18% penal interest, e-way bill blocking, and loss of Input Tax Credit (ITC) for your B2B customers.

Our Chartered Accountancy practice provides end-to-end management: from swift portal registration and HSN classification to automated invoice reconciliations under GSTR-2B, ensuring your business claims every single rupee of eligible tax credit.

Who Needs GST Registration?

  • ✓ Turnover Threshold: Businesses with annual aggregate turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs (Goods) or ₹20 Lakhs (Services).
  • ✓ Inter-State Suppliers: Any entity selling goods or services across state borders must register regardless of turnover.
  • ✓ E-Commerce Sellers: Vendors selling via Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, or operating digital marketplace portals.
  • ✓ Export Oriented Units & Tech Services: IT exporters requiring GST registration for LUT filing to enjoy 0% tax exports.

Documents Required for GST Registration

  • PAN Card of Proprietor / Company / LLP
  • Aadhaar Card of Promoters / Partners / Directors
  • Proof of Business Address (Electricity Bill / Property Tax Receipt)
  • Rent Agreement and NOC from landlord
  • Cancelled Cheque or Bank Statement showing Name & IFSC
  • Partnership Deed or Certificate of Incorporation (for LLP/Companies)
  • Passport size photographs of authorized signatories
  • Letter of Authorization / Board Resolution for authorized signatory
